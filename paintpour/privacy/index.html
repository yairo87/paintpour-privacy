Privacy Policy

Paint Pour · Last updated 13 November 2026

Paint Pour has no account and no sign-in, and your progress stays on your phone. The game is free and paid for by ads. The ad service it uses, AppLovin, collects some information from your phone to show them. This page says what, and how to limit it.

What the game stores, and where

The game keeps one file in its own folder on your phone, holding:

  • your progress: the furthest level open and your best stars on each level
  • your coins, your lives and when the next one comes back, and the hints, extra vials and drains you own
  • a log of your last 500 attempts: the level, how it ended, and how many pours, undos, stars and seconds it took. It helps us tune how hard the levels are while the game is being tested. Nothing sends it anywhere.
  • whether you bought Remove ads or the Starter pack, and when you last bought something, since ads between levels pause for a day after a purchase
  • counters that space out the ads: when the last one between levels played, how many levels you have finished since, how many played today, and when you last watched one for a free hint
  • your settings: sound and vibration

The file is removed when you delete the game. If your phone backs up apps to your Google account, Android may include it in that backup, which Google keeps under the Google Privacy Policy.

What leaves your phone

What is described here, and nothing else. The game contains no analytics or crash reporting of our own, and we run no server that receives anything from it.

Ads. Now and then an ad plays when you go on to the next level, and you can choose to watch an ad for a reward, such as a free hint or more pours. Remove ads stops the ones between levels. The ads are served by AppLovin MAX, from AppLovin Corp. To pick an ad, show it, count it and guard against fraud, the AppLovin library inside the game sends AppLovin:

  • your phone's advertising ID, and device details such as its model, Android version, language and time zone
  • your IP address, from which a rough location, such as your country or city, can be worked out
  • which ads were shown and what you did with them: whether you tapped one, and whether you watched a rewarded ad to the end
  • technical logs from the ad library, such as errors and load times

The game gives AppLovin nothing else: not your progress, coins or purchases. AppLovin handles what it collects under its own privacy policy. AppLovin MAX can also ask other ad networks for an ad. When we add one, it is named here with a link to its own policy.

Purchases. Coins, helpers, the Starter pack and Remove ads are bought through Google Play, which handles the payment entirely: the game never sees your card or payment details. To know what is already yours, the game asks Google Play which one-time purchases the Google account on this phone owns, when it starts and when you tap Restore purchases. After a purchase it confirms it back to Google Play, which Play requires so the purchase is not refunded. Google shares with us the order records it provides to every developer for payments and refunds. Google describes its own handling of purchases in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks before ads can use your data to be personalised, with a consent form provided by Google. Google's servers use your IP address to tell whether the form applies to you; Google describes this in how Google uses information from apps that use its services. If you say no, ads still play but are not personalised; they may still use data to count views, limit repeats and catch fraud. You can change your answer at any time from Privacy choices in the game's Settings.

Everywhere, you can delete or reset your phone's advertising ID in Android's settings, on most phones under Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads. Once it is deleted, ads can no longer be personalised from it.

In US states with privacy laws, such as California, letting an ad network use your advertising ID for personalised ads can count as "sharing" or "selling" personal information. Deleting your advertising ID as above opts you out. We do not sell anything ourselves, since nothing about you reaches us.

Your data and your rights

We keep nothing about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to hand over or delete: deleting the game deletes everything it stored. AppLovin's privacy policy explains how to see or delete what it holds. If you cannot get there, write to us and we will help you reach AppLovin.
